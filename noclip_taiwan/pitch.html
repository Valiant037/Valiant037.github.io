04
The Tech & Experience
MOONDREAMREALITY
100 Players. 10x30 Meters. True Scale.

We leverage colossal Location-Based VR (LBE) to create a physically real multiplayer sandbox. Players move freely in the physical space while exploring miles of virtual corridors.

PHYSICAL SPACE
10x30m Arena
Designed with dynamic physical partitions matching virtual walls, enabling tactile feedback and safe, high-density exploration.
SOCIAL PANIC
100-Player Unity
Proximity voice chat lets players hear distant screams or organize panic huddles. Mass hysteria becomes a gameplay mechanic.
THE MONSTER
Dynamic Entity AI
The Entity hunts using sound and visual light detection. It is fast, faceless, and thrives on splitting groups apart.

Selling the Ideas
MOONDREAMREALITY
Why Players Will Queue For This
USP A
Social-Horror Spectacle
Most VR horror is a lonely, isolated experience. This is a massive survival social experiment where your best chance of survival is using your group as sound-decoys or coordinate distractions.
USP B
Tactile & Visual Realism
Utilizing photorealistic assets of local Taiwanese architecture combined with industrial-grade haptic flooring and wind/humidity emitters to blur the line of digital and physical space.
USP C
The "YouTube Sensationalism" Loop
Integrating a "Spectator Mode" where content creators outside the arena can control the environment, toggle lights, or trigger entities, driving massive online streamability and viral loops.
USP D
High Replayability
The layout of the liminal maze shifts dynamically based on group choices and performance, ensuring that no two runs of the Backrooms are identical.

07
Branching Realities
MOONDREAMREALITY
DYNAMIC STORYTELLING
FAMILIAR STREETS.
INFINITE VARIATIONS.

To drive repeat play rate (a critical metric for LBE profitability), players' actions and failures branch the physical maze in real-time. No two runs are identical, leading players to navigate completely different spaces depending on who they save, what doors they unlock, and how they evade the Entity.

PATHWAY 01 // COWARDICE
The Sacrificial Route
Sacrificing team members to the Entity unlocks shortcut fire escapes, leading to an early, tragic ending.
PATHWAY 02 // LOGIC
The Facility Breach
Deciphering MRT flickering terminal codes reveals hidden utility elevators, unlocking the underground laboratory sector.
PATHWAY 03 // HEROISM
The Noclip Exit
Reaching the temples together under red light unlocks the fabled 'Noclip' glitch exit back to the real Taiwan.
